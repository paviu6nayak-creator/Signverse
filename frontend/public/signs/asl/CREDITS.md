# Sign Language Asset Credits & Licensing

All vector graphics and reference illustrations located in `public/signs/asl/` adhere strictly to public-domain (CC0 1.0 Universal) or Open Cultural Educational standards:

### 1. Manual Alphabet (A-Z) & Numbers (0-9)
- **Source standard**: American Sign Language (ASL) fingerspelling and hand counting conventions as documented by Gallaudet University, the National Association of the Deaf (NAD), and Wikimedia Commons ASL Alphabet repository.
- **License**: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication).
- **Format**: Vector SVGs designed with consistent dark/neon palettes matching the SignVerse Aurora design system.

### 2. Expanded Numbers (10-20, Tens 30-90, Compounds 21-99, 100, 1000)
- **Source standard**: ASL counting principles verified via Dr. Bill Vicars (Lifeprint / ASLU) and the National Association of the Deaf (NAD).
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- **Usage**: Free for non-commercial educational instruction.

### 3. Core Vocabulary & Common Signs
- **Source standard**: ASL lexical signs (Hello, Thank You, Please, Sorry, Help, Water, Food, Family, Friend, etc.) verified against Dr. Bill Vicars (Lifeprint ASL University).
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal.

### 4. Compound Phrases & Sequential Sentences
- **Concept**: Sequential multi-token combination diagrams broken down into distinct sub-tokens.
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal.
